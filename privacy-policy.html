Privacy Policy for Phone Cleaner

Effective Date: September 23, 2026

Phone Cleaner ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Phone Cleaner handles information when you use the application.

1. Information We Access

Phone Cleaner may access files, images, videos, audio files, documents, downloads, and other storage information on your device when you use features that require such access.

This access is used to provide features such as storage analysis, file management, duplicate-file detection, media management, and cleaning or optimization features.

Phone Cleaner does not access files that are not necessary for the feature you are using.

2. How Your Files Are Handled

Files and media accessed by Phone Cleaner are processed on your device for the application's features unless a feature explicitly indicates otherwise.

We do not sell your personal files, photos, videos, documents, or other private content.

3. Personal Information

Phone Cleaner does not require you to create an account or provide your name, phone number, address, or other personal information to use the core cleaning features.

4. Advertising

Phone Cleaner may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising services, such as Google Mobile Ads (AdMob).

Advertising providers may collect certain information, such as advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location, and information related to advertising interactions, subject to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

We do not sell your personal information to advertisers.

5. Analytics and Device Information

The application or its third-party services may process limited technical information necessary to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the application. This may include device type, operating system version, application version, and diagnostic information.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Information may be processed by third-party service providers only when necessary to provide services such as advertising, diagnostics, security, or application functionality.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Phone Cleaner does not maintain a user account or a server-side personal-data profile for its core cleaning functionality. Information stored locally on your device remains under your control and can be removed by uninstalling the application or deleting the relevant files through your device.

9. Children's Privacy

Phone Cleaner is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Third-Party Services

Third-party services used by the application may have their own privacy policies. Users should review the privacy policies of those services for information about how they handle data.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of Phone Cleaner, please contact the developer through the contact information provided on the application's Google Play listing.

13. Your Choices

You can control application permissions through your Android device settings. You may allow or deny permissions according to the options provided by Android.
